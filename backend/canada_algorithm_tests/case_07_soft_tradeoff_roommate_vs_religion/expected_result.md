# Expected Result — Soft Trade-off: Roommate vs Religion

- 20 students and 20 gender-compatible beds.
- Four female mutual roommate pairs deliberately cross ordinary religion groups.
- `roommateMatch` weight = 10.
- `sameReligion` weight = 3.
- Other soft preferences are disabled in this case to isolate the trade-off.
- Hard gender and hard religious-placement rules remain active.
- All 20 students should be assigned.
- At least **3 of the 4** mutual roommate pairs should share an apartment.

This case demonstrates optimization between two soft preferences rather than a hard-rule test.
